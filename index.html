Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Google My Business and Google Business Profile?

They are the same tool. Google renamed Google My Business to Google Business Profile. Many people still use the old name, so we use both.

Can you get my business to the top of Google Maps?

No honest agency can guarantee a position. We improve everything within your control and report clearly on results.

Which parts of London do you cover?

All 32 London boroughs and the City of London, including their main neighbourhoods and postcode areas.

Do you work with businesses that have several locations?

Yes. We can manage multiple Google Business Profiles and build a page for each location and area.

How quickly will I see results?

Some profile fixes show effects within days. Meaningful ranking improvement usually takes weeks or months, depending on your competition.

Do you offer help if my profile is suspended?

Yes. We look into the cause, prepare evidence and help you submit a reinstatement request.
